3. Key Concurrency & Reliability Highlights

All race conditions are solved inside PostgreSQL transactions using transaction-scoped advisory locks:

SELECT pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('host_lock_' || host_id));

Concurrency stress test verified: 20 simultaneous hold requests on a capacity=1 slot resulted in exactly 1 success and 19 rejections (409 Conflict).

4. Daylight Saving Time Worked Example

  • EDT (Oct 30, 2026): 09:00 AM EDT = 13:00:00Z
  • EST (Nov 02, 2026): 09:00 AM EST = 14:00:00Z

Automatically handled via IANA timezone conversions, ensuring zero booking drift across daylight saving transitions.
